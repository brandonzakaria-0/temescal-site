How {{brand}} began

It started close to home.

Brandon Zakaria grew up in Los Angeles and went to Palisades Charter High School. On January 7, 2025, the Palisades Fire started on the ridge above Temescal Canyon. Before it was contained, it had burned part of his old high school and destroyed his aunt's home.

Across the Palisades, families were left with practical questions at the hardest possible time: what their policy actually covered, what it would cost to rebuild today, and whether anyone would insure the home once it was built.

At Stanford, where he was finishing his MBA, Brandon set out to understand why insuring a home in wildfire country had become so hard. In fall 2026 he had about sixty conversations with brokers, underwriters, insurers, realtors, and homeowners in hillside towns like Woodside, Portola Valley, and Los Altos Hills. He also went to ITC Vegas, the insurance industry's largest technology conference, to see how the industry itself was trying to change.

So in October 2026 he earned his California Property and Casualty license and started {{brand}}. He named it for the canyon where the fire began.
