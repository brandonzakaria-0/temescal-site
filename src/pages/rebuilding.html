What changes

A new home deserves a fresh look at coverage.

Built to newer standards.

Insurers look closely at roofs, vents, and the space around a home. What you build in can open more markets.

Rebuild value, reconsidered.

Custom construction costs can exceed what your previous policy covered. Limits should match the home you're building.

Needs that change as you build.

Coverage during construction and coverage after you move in are different conversations. Start both early.

Coverage during and after construction.

During construction

A home under construction is usually covered by a builder's risk policy, not a homeowners policy. Ask your builder what their policy covers, and talk with your broker early about the switch to a homeowners policy before you move in.

When you move in

A homeowners policy matched to your new home's rebuild value, shopped across every market, with the hardening you've built in documented for insurers.
