Coverage

What is the FAIR Plan?

California's insurer of last resort. It mainly covers fire, so most homeowners pair it with a companion policy (called a DIC policy) for theft, water damage, liability, and more.

What is surplus lines insurance?

Coverage from insurers that aren't licensed by California but are allowed to cover homes that licensed insurers decline. It can offer broader coverage, but it isn't backed by California's insurance guarantee fund, and it's often more expensive. You'll sign a state notice (form D-1) before placement. We'll explain the tradeoffs before you decide.

Will hardening my home lower my premium?

Often it helps. California requires licensed insurers to offer discounts for qualifying mitigation, and hardening can make a home eligible for more insurers. Results vary by insurer and home.

What is an insurability report?

A short report on your home's wildfire features, which insurers it qualifies for today, and which improvements could open more options or earn discounts. See a sample.
