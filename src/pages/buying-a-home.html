Why it matters

Why insurance can hold up a closing.

It's a condition of the loan.

Lenders won't fund without proof that the home is insured from the day you take ownership.

Fewer insurers are writing new homes.

In wildfire areas, finding an insurer can mean looking across several markets at once: standard insurers, the FAIR Plan with a companion policy, and surplus lines.

High-value homes need the right limits.

Rebuild costs for custom homes can exceed the limits many policies offer, so coverage has to be matched to the home, not just found.

Lender requirements

What lenders typically ask for.

Requirements vary by lender. Ask your loan officer for theirs, or have them send it to us.

  • Proof of a homeowners policy, or a binder, effective on your closing date
  • Dwelling coverage that meets the lender's minimum, often tied to the loan amount or the home's replacement cost
  • The lender named on the policy as mortgagee
  • The first year's premium paid at or before closing
