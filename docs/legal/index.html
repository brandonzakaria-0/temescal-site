Our role

Temescal is an insurance broker, not an insurer. We represent you and place coverage with insurers. Policies are issued by those insurers, and coverage is subject to each insurer's terms and approval. We can't promise that any insurer will offer coverage for a particular home, or the price or timing of any offer.

Surplus lines disclosure

Some homes are placed with surplus lines (non-admitted) insurers. These insurers are not licensed by California, and policies they issue are not protected by California's insurance guarantee fund if the insurer becomes insolvent. Before placement, California requires a diligent search of licensed insurers. You'll receive and sign a state notice (form D-1), and you'll have five days to cancel after receiving it.

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Temescal Insurance ("Temescal," "we") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you request a review, refer a client, or book a call, you may give us your name, email, phone number, property address, details about the home and your situation, dates such as a closing or policy end date, your current insurer, and documents you upload, such as a declarations page or a non-renewal notice.
  • Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider records basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, to deliver and protect the site. If you arrive through a link that includes campaign details, we note that source with your request. We do not use advertising cookies or track you across other websites.

How we use it

To review your home, find and compare coverage options, place and service coverage you choose, contact you about your request, keep the records California requires of licensed insurance producers, and keep this site working.

How we share it

We share personal information as needed to do that work:

  • with insurers, and with other brokers and agents we work with, when we look for coverage on your behalf
  • with service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting, scheduling, form handling, and email
  • with the realtor, lender, or advisor who referred you, if you agree
  • when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Other services on this site

Our booking calendar is provided by Calendly, and our fonts by Google Fonts. These services receive basic technical information from your browser when a page loads, and they have their own privacy policies.

Do Not Track signals

We do not track visitors across other websites, so this site works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. We keep it as long as we need it for the purposes above and as long as insurance record-keeping rules require.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete information we are not required to keep. You can stop calls or texts at any time by replying STOP or by telling us.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. Questions or requests: brandon@temescalinsurance.com, (310) 415-8600.
