Every market, side by side

Three ways to insure a home in California. We compare all of them.

Insurers issue the policies. Your broker works for you, shops each market that might cover your home, and explains the tradeoffs before you decide.

Standard (admitted)

Standard homeowners policy

Full coverage from an insurer licensed by California, including private client insurers that specialize in high-value homes.

When it fits
The best outcome when an insurer will write the home, often after hardening.
Good to know
Eligible for California's wildfire mitigation discounts, and backed by the state's insurance guarantee fund.
Two policies together

FAIR Plan plus a companion policy

A fire policy from the FAIR Plan, California's insurer of last resort, paired with a companion policy (called DIC) that fills the gaps.

When it fits
Homes standard insurers won't write today.
Good to know
Two policies, two bills. FAIR Plan limits can fall short of a high-value home's rebuild value.
Surplus lines (E&S)

Surplus lines homeowners policy

Full coverage from an insurer not licensed by California but allowed to cover homes licensed insurers decline. More flexible, often more expensive.

When it fits
Higher-value or hard-to-place homes, or when the FAIR Plan pairing falls short.
Good to know
Not backed by California's insurance guarantee fund if the insurer fails. You sign a state notice (form D-1) before placement.
Your insurability report

A policy for this year. A plan for next year.

Every client receives a short report on their home: the wildfire features insurers look at, which markets the home qualifies for today, and which hardening steps could open more markets or earn discounts.

  • Roof class, vents, and the 5-foot ember-resistant zone around the house
  • Vegetation, defensible space, and the home's fire hazard zone
  • Steps recognized by California's Safer from Wildfires program and the IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home designation

Questions

What people ask first.

All questions
Are you an insurance company?

No. We're an independent, licensed brokerage. We work for you and shop many insurers to find coverage that fits.

Do you use AI? Who looks at my application?

Our software handles the paperwork so we can move fast. A licensed broker reviews every application and every recommendation, and you can always talk to a person.

I got a non-renewal notice. What should I do?

Don't wait until the last week. Send us the notice and your address, and we'll start looking at every option while you still have time.

What is the FAIR Plan?

California's insurer of last resort. It mainly covers fire, so most homeowners pair it with a companion policy (called a DIC policy) for theft, water damage, liability, and more.

How quickly will I hear back?

Your broker reviews every request personally. If you have a closing or policy end date, tell us and we'll prioritize it.
